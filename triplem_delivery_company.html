Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a tracking number?

Contact the company to arrange your shipment. Once it is accepted and registered, ask the team for its tracking reference.

Why is my tracking number not showing a result?

Check that you entered the number correctly. If it still does not work, contact customer support. In this demo, tracking records exist only in the browser where they were created.

Can I request a delivery quote?

Yes. Send your pickup location, destination, parcel size or description and preferred date through the enquiry form or WhatsApp.

Can I pay on this website?

No. This website intentionally has no payment option. Confirm delivery arrangements and any payment instructions directly with the company.
